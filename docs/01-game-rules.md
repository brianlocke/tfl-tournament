# TFL Official Game Rules

Source of truth for gameplay flow. Card-specific scoring effects live in `07-action-cards.md`. Final scoring math lives in `06-scoring-logic.md`.

## Object of the Game

- Score more points than your opponent
- First player to **exceed 30 points** wins
- Play one game or multiple games / tournaments

## Setup

- Choose teams: Red vs Blue
- Each player places Action Card Deck face down on own pile
- Each player selects **3 Action Cards** from top of deck, places in card holder
- Flip football to determine first possession (Red side up = Red goes first)

## Starting Play

- Starting player places football on **Start Line (1-yard line)** and flicks
- Players alternate one flick per turn
- **No pushing** — football must be flicked or tapped with fingers
- After any score, football resets to scorer's own 1-yard line

## Gameplay Rules

### Out of Bounds
If any part of the football touches or crosses a yellow boundary, it is out of bounds. Football resets to opposing player's own 30-yard line; their turn.

### Touchdown — 6 points
Any part of the football crosses the goal line without going out of bounds.

### Extra Point — 1 point
After a TD, football placed at the 30-yard line. Opposing player places Goal Post on TFL logo in end zone. Player flicks football through goalpost.

### Field Goal — 3 points
Player flicks football inside Red Zone (Opp 20-yard line); may immediately attempt FG from 30-yard line. Missed FG → football restarts on own 30-yard line.

## Action Cards (overview)

- A new Action Card can only be activated after crossing into the Red Zone (Opp 20-yard line)
- Active card stays in effect until resolved or replaced
- Cannot be played immediately after Extra Point or Field Goal (exception: Blocked Field Goal)
- Each card can only be used one time; afterward it's discarded next turn
- If deck depleted, shuffle discard pile to form new deck

> Full card catalog with scoring impact is in `07-action-cards.md`.

## Standard Scoring Summary (no cards)

| Event | Points |
|---|---|
| Touchdown | 6 |
| Extra Point (kick) | 1 |
| Field Goal | 3 |

> Card-modified scoring events (Super TD, Super FG, Extra Points card) are documented in `06-scoring-logic.md`.
